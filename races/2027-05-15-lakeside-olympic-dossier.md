## Conditions (historical mid-May)

- Air at start: 58–64°F, rising to 72–78°F by the run
- Water: 64–68°F, wetsuit legal
- Humidity: moderate. Sun low and to the right on the swim's back straight.

## Aid stations

- Bike: one bottle exchange at the turnaround (mi 12.4), sports drink + water
- Run: every ~1.5 mi (4 stations), water, sports drink, gels at stations 2 and 4

## Course notes

- Bike: the two climbs are where the HR cap matters. Shift early, hold Z4 ceiling (164), don't chase speed uphill.
- Run: gravel sections are loop miles 1.4–2.3. Hold effort, let pace drop.
