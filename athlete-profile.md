## Coaching Philosophy

Built from three sources:

- **Stephen Seiler (polarized training):** ~80% of sessions genuinely easy, ~20%
  genuinely hard, very little in Zone 3.
- **Joe Friel (The Triathlete's Training Bible):** Base → Build → Peak → Race
  periodization, LTHR-based zones, aerobic decoupling as the signal the base
  is ready.
- **Matt Dixon (Purple Patch):** training is one stress among many. Sleep,
  post-workout fueling and life stress decide whether training sticks. Key
  sessions are protected; supporting sessions flex.

### Standing rules

- One true rest day per week.
- Carbohydrate + protein within 30 minutes of every session.
- Every 3rd week in Base and Build is a recovery week (−40% volume, intensity stays).
- Easy days are Zone 2 with a hard ceiling at 147 bpm. Above it, walk.
- The 3-week trend is the truth, not one bad session.
