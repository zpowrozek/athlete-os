# 15-Week Macro Plan: Lakeside Olympic Triathlon

> **Demo data** for a fictional athlete. `plan-my-week` reads this file to know
> which phase a week falls in and what that phase calls for.

**Block:** Mon 2027-02-01 → Sun 2027-05-16 (105 days)
**Race:** Sat 2027-05-15, 7:30 AM wave
**Race distances:** 1,640 yd swim / 24.8 mi bike / 6.2 mi run

---

## Phase overview

| Weeks | Dates | Phase | Weekly endurance volume | Intensity split |
|-------|-------|-------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| 1–6 | Feb 1 – Mar 14 | Base | 240 → 320 min | 85–90% Z1–2, 0 threshold |
| 7–11 | Mar 15 – Apr 18 | Build | 300 → 380 min | 75–80% Z1–2, 1–2 Z4 sessions |
| 12–14 | Apr 19 – May 9 | Peak / Taper | −47%, race sim, −50% | Intensity stays, volume drops |
| 15 | May 10 – May 16 | Race Week | ~50 min of shake-outs | Primers only |

Recovery weeks: **Week 3, Week 6, Week 9** (−40% volume, one quality session kept).

---

## Base (Weeks 1–6)

**Goal:** aerobic foundation, swim frequency, run easy for real.

- Swim 3×/week, technique first (bilateral breathing, high-elbow catch, sighting).
- Run 2×/week in Zone 2, walk breaks whenever HR passes 147.
- Bike 2×/week, one Zone 2, one longer Zone 2.
- Strength: A (gym) + B (glute/core) + C (swim shoulder), 3 sessions/week.
- Milestone: aerobic decoupling < 5% on a 90-min Zone 2 ride → ready for Build.

## Build (Weeks 7–11)

**Goal:** race-specific fitness and threshold work.

- Swim 3×/week; one race-pace set (e.g. 6×100 yd @ goal pace, 20 s rest).
- Bike: one cruise-interval session (3–4 × 8–10 min Z4 @ 90–95 rpm), one long ride.
- Run: one Zone 2 run, one brick run off the Saturday ride.
- Bricks start Week 8 (bike → run, 15–25 min run).
- Strength: A + B, 2 sessions/week.
- Milestones: 1,500 yd continuous swim; 26 mi ride at Z2; 5.5 mi run without walking.

## Peak / Taper (Weeks 12–14)

- Week 12: −47% from Week 11. One sharp session per discipline.
- Week 13: race simulation brick on Saturday (full bike + 4 mi run at race effort).
- Week 14: −50% from Week 13. Race-pace primers (4–6 × 20–30 s) in each discipline.
- No new sessions, distances or equipment.

## Race Week (Week 15)

- Tue: swim shake-out (15 min + 4 × 25 yd race pace).
- Wed: bike shake-out + mechanical check.
- Thu: run shake-out (15 min + 4 strides).
- Fri: rest, packet pickup, course recon.
- Sat: race. Wake 4:30, breakfast 4:45 (~100 g carbs), transition opens 6:00.
