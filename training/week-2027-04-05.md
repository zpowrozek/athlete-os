## Session Details

### Tue Apr 6 — Swim, race pace ★ [HARD]

**Strength C first (12 min, band):** pull-aparts 3×15, face pulls 3×15, external rotation 3×12 each, prone Y-T-W 2×10.

**Swim — 45 min, ~1,500 yd**
- Warmup: 300 yd easy, bilateral breathing every 3
- Drill: 4 × 50 yd high-elbow catch with pull buoy, 15 s rest
- **Main: 6 × 100 yd @ 2:30/100yd (A-goal race pace), 20 s rest**
- Cooldown: 200 yd easy, sighting every 4th length

*Why it's here:* Race pace is the swim's only Z4 work this week. Six reps is the first time the full set lands at goal pace; Week 8 managed four.

### Wed Apr 7 — Strength B + Run, Zone 2

**Strength B (15 min, before the run):** glute bridges 3×15, clamshells 3×15 each, lateral band walks 3×20 steps, dead bug 3×10, side plank 3×30 s each.

**Run — 45 min, Zone 2 (HR 134–147, aim 135–142)**
- 5 min walk + run warmup (leg swings, A-skips, 4 × 20 s strides)
- 35 min easy. **If HR passes 147, walk until it's back under 140.**
- 5 min walk cooldown

*Why it's here:* Easy aerobic volume between two hard days. Strength B first so the glutes are firing before the run, which is where the late-run fade has shown up.

### Thu Apr 8 — Bike, cruise intervals ★ [HARD]

**60 min, trainer or flat loop**
- Warmup: 15 min Z1–2 @ 85 rpm, last 3 min building to Z3
- **Main: 3 × 10 min Z4 (HR 155–164) @ 90–95 rpm, 3 min Z1 spin between**
- Cooldown: 6 min easy @ 85 rpm

*Why it's here:* Friel's cruise intervals are the Build phase workhorse. Week 8 was 3 × 8 min; +2 min per rep is the progression, with the same rep count.

### Fri Apr 9 — Swim, endurance + Strength A

**Swim — 40 min, ~1,350 yd:** 200 warmup, **3 × 350 yd Z2 continuous, bilateral breathing, 30 s rest**, 100 cooldown.

**Strength A (25 min, gym, Build phase 3×12):** goblet squat, Romanian deadlift, single-leg RDL, reverse lunge, lat pulldown, plank 3×45 s.

*Why it's here:* Continuous yardage toward the 1,640 yd race swim. Strength A sits on an easy day, two days clear of Thursday's intervals.

### Sat Apr 10 — Brick ★

**Bike — 90 min, Zone 2 (134–147), 80–90 rpm.** Shift before cadence drops under 75 on climbs. Fuel 40–50 g carbs/hour from minute 20.

**Run off the bike — 25 min, Zone 2.** First 5 min will feel heavy; hold HR, not pace. Walk if HR passes 147.

*Why it's here:* Longest brick of the block so far (Week 8 was 75 + 20). Practises the bike-to-run transition at race-day fueling rates.
